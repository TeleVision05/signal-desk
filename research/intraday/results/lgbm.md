# LightGBM triple-barrier classifier (family `lgbm`, kind `model`)

Script: `experiments/intraday/lgbm.py`. Long/flat spot, BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, walk-forward OOS window
[2020-01-01, 2026-07-01), every config logged at 5 and 10 bps/side, seed 7. Bars: 5m cache resampled
with `incomplete="drop"` (drops 12 / 28 / 48 incomplete 15m / 1h / 4h bars over 2017-09..2026-06).

## Pre-registration (written 2026-11-05 before any OOS run)

12 configs per symbol, 24 runs in total (x2 costs). No config will be added after seeing OOS results
unless it is logged and marked `post_hoc` below.

Grid: timeframe x vertical barrier `h` x barrier multiples. `h` is in bars (15m: 16 = 4h, 96 = 1 day;
1h: 6 = 6h, 24 = 1 day; 4h: 6 = 1 day, 30 = 5 days). Barriers are `entry * exp(+pt * sigma)` and
`entry * exp(-sl * sigma)`, `sigma` = std of 1-bar log close returns over 7 days of bars x sqrt(h)
(`labels.triple_barrier`, vol_window = 7 days in bars).

Fixed for every config (not tuned):

* **Label**: `y = 1` if the triple-barrier realised log return > 10 bps (one base-cost round trip),
  else 0. Rows whose barrier is not resolved inside the data are unlabelled.
* **Sample weights**: average label uniqueness (de Prado), computed per outer fold from the fold's
  own purged training labels only (concurrency counts the spans `[t+1, t1)` of training labels;
  every one ends before the test block, so the weight is causal). Normalised to mean 1.
* **Model**: `LGBMClassifier(n_estimators=200, learning_rate=0.05, num_leaves=15, max_depth=4,
  min_child_samples=500, subsample=0.7, subsample_freq=1, colsample_bytree=0.7, reg_lambda=10,
  reg_alpha=1, min_split_gain=0.0, random_state=7, deterministic=True, force_row_wise=True,
  n_jobs=4)`.
* **Features** (windows scaled to the timeframe, D = bars per day): log returns over
  1, D/6, D, 7D, 30D bars; realized vol over D and 7D; return z-score (horizon D/6, window 7D); taker
  imbalance over 1 and D; VWAP deviation over D and 7D; volume ratio (D/6 vs 7D); bar range and close
  location; calendar (hour / day-of-week, cyclical); cross-asset returns and relative returns over 1,
  D and 7D; plus two script-local causal features: log(close / EMA(7D)) and log(close / EMA(30D)),
  and log(rv_D / rv_7D).
* **Walk-forward**: `splits.walk_forward_splits`, expanding window from 2017-09-01, purged by
  triple-barrier `t1`, 1-day embargo. 1h and 4h retrain MONTHLY (78 test months); 15m retrains
  QUARTERLY (26 test quarters).
  * Amendment (2026-11-05, before any OOS result was produced or logged): the first registration said
    monthly for every timeframe. The first logged run was killed after 11 minutes without finishing
    its first 15m walk-forward (machine load average ~89 on 12 cores from parallel agents, so a single
    300k-row fit was far slower than the ~1.2 s measured idle). Per the task's compute rule, 15m was
    coarsened to quarterly retraining and the run order changed to 4h, 1h, 15m. Nothing had been
    logged to `lgbm.jsonl` at that point, and no OOS number had been printed.
* **Decision threshold**: tuned per outer fold on a 4-fold `splits.purged_kfold(..., within=fold)`
  over the fold's training rows. Candidate thresholds are the 20%, 25%, ..., 95% quantiles of the
  out-of-fold probabilities; the objective is the summed net log P&L of `target = p > thr` held bar
  by bar (each row earns `log(open[t+2]/open[t+1])`, minus 5 bps per unit of position change, each
  validation block starting flat). If no candidate has positive net P&L the fold is flat for the
  month. The chosen absolute probability threshold is applied to the final model refitted on all
  training rows.
* **Position**: `target[t] = 1` if `p_t > thr` else 0, flat where features are missing. Stitched
  OOS positions are backtested once over [EVAL_START, EVAL_END) at 5 and 10 bps/side.

## Verdict (written after the run; no config was added or re-run after seeing these results)

**No, it does not beat the best baseline, and it only barely and inconsistently beats buy-and-hold.**
The best config on the weaker symbol is `lgbm_4h_h30_pt2_sl1`: BTC 0.95 / 0.64 and ETH 0.82 / 0.62
net Sharpe at 5 / 10 bps, against the registry's best baselines of 1.35 (BTC) and 1.47 (ETH) and
buy-and-hold at 0.84 (BTC) and 0.89 (ETH). It beats buy-and-hold on BTC by 0.11 and loses to it on
ETH; no config beats both. Its drawdowns (-69% / -72%) are nearly buy-and-hold sized and far worse
than the -38% / -39% of `ema_px_4h_180`. With 252 runs in the registry the DSR bar is out of reach
anyway, so `promotion.evaluate` was not run; it would fail on Sharpe >= 1.0, MaxDD, positive months,
worst year and the baseline comparison on both symbols.

**Where the edge comes from:** it is long beta plus a noisy copy of the trend rule, not a new
signal. Over the OOS window the best config's daily P&L correlates 0.74 (BTC) / 0.80 (ETH) with
buy-and-hold and 0.68 with `ema_px_4h_180`, with a beta of 0.55 / 0.64 to buy-and-hold, and earns
about as much on buy-and-hold's up days as it loses on the down days (BTC +137 / -117 bps, ETH
+202 / -185 bps per day): no asymmetric timing. The final models lean on the 30-day return, 7-day
realized vol and the distance from the 30-day EMA, i.e. the same information the 4h trend baseline
uses, but the classifier's probability crosses its threshold far more often: 0.37 to 0.65 trades a
day at 4h against 0.09 for the baseline, so the net edge per trade falls from about 190 bps (baseline)
to 16 to 41 bps. The inner-CV thresholds sit low (median chosen quantile 0.25 to 0.40 at 4h), so the
4h models are long 57 to 66% of the time, which is why they track the market.

**Cost and timeframe sensitivity:** strongly monotone in timeframe, 4h > 1h > 15m. Every 4h config
keeps a positive Sharpe at 10 bps (0.10 to 0.64, a 0.2 to 0.5 drop from 5 bps). Every 1h config is at
or below zero at 10 bps (best 0.05) and earns 3 to 10 bps a trade at 5 bps, so its edge is roughly one
cost level wide. At 15m nothing works: the inner purged CV found no profitable threshold in 10 to 25
of the 26 quarters (the model stays flat), and the quarters where it did trade lost money at both
costs (Sharpe -0.58 to +0.37 at 5 bps, all negative at 10 bps). The triple-barrier label shape
matters less than timeframe: pt 2 / sl 1 with a 5-day vertical barrier is best at 4h, but the
spread between the four 4h configs (min-symbol Sharpe 0.59 to 0.82) is within what a 24-run grid
produces by chance.

**Disaster years:** yes. Every 1h and 4h config lost 21% to 73% in 2022 (best config: BTC -40%,
ETH -39%), and seven of the eight 4h runs lost again in 2025 and the first half of 2026 (best config BTC
-42% then -34%, ETH -22% then -26%). Performance is concentrated in 2020-2021 and decays afterwards,
the shape expected from a model that is mostly long beta in a bull market. Worst calendar year is
between -27% and -73% for every 1h and 4h run, against a -10% promotion floor.

**Process notes:** 15m was coarsened to quarterly retraining before any OOS run (see the amendment
above); 1h and 4h retrained monthly. The threshold was tuned at 5 bps only, which is part of why the
stress-cost results degrade: a cost-aware turnover penalty (hysteresis, minimum hold) was not in the
pre-registered grid and was not tried.
