# L2 logistic regression (family `logreg`, kind `model`)

Script: `experiments/intraday/logreg.py`. Long/flat spot, BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, walk-forward OOS
window [2020-01-01, 2026-07-01), every config logged at 5 and 10 bps/side, seed 7. Bars: 5m cache
resampled with `incomplete="drop"` (12 / 28 / 48 incomplete 15m / 1h / 4h bars dropped over
2017-09..2026-06), then inner-joined with the other symbol's bars (`features.align`) so the
cross-asset features never see a missing bar; labels and the backtest run on that aligned frame.

## Pre-registration (written 2026-11-05 before any OOS run)

24 configs per symbol, 48 runs in total (x2 costs). No config will be added after seeing OOS
results unless it is logged and marked `post_hoc` below.

**Fixed for every config (not tuned):**

| item | value |
|---|---|
| model | `StandardScaler` -> `LogisticRegression(penalty="l2", solver="lbfgs", max_iter=500, random_state=7)`; the scaler is refit inside every (inner and outer) training fold |
| outer walk-forward | `splits.walk_forward_splits(train="rolling", train_window=24 months)`, monthly test blocks 2020-01..2026-06, purged by label `t1`, 1-day embargo. **Rolling 24 months** (pre-registered; expanding is not run) |
| inner CV (per outer fold) | `splits.purged_kfold(n_splits=4, within=fold)` on that fold's training rows only |
| tuned per outer fold | `C` in {0.0005, 0.005, 0.05} x entry threshold `thr` in {0.50, 0.51, 0.52, 0.54, 0.56, 0.58, 0.60} (21 pairs) |
| selection objective | pooled mean NET bps per trade at 5 bps/side over the 4 inner validation blocks, each block simulated with `backtest.run_backtest` using the config's band, `min_hold_bars` and `max_round_trips_per_day`; a pair needs >= 20 pooled trades. If no pair has a positive pooled net mean, the model stays **flat** for that test month |
| position rule | hysteresis (no-trade band): enter long when p > thr, stay long while p >= thr - band, else flat; then `min_hold_bars` and `max_round_trips_per_day` applied by the simulator on executed positions |
| features | harness library (`make_feature_frame`, windows scaled by bars/day d = 96 / 24 / 6 for 15m / 1h / 4h): log_returns (1, 4, d, 7d), realized_vol (d, 7d), return_zscore (h 4, window 30d), taker_imbalance (1, d), vwap_deviation (d, 7d), volume_ratio (max(2, d/4), 7d), range_location, calendar, cross_asset vs the other symbol (1, d, 7d); plus script-local causal trend features: log(close / EMA(span k*d)) for k = 1, 7, 30; 30-day log return; log(close / rolling 30-day max close); log(rv_d / rv_30d). 30 features. Rows with any non-finite feature are not trained on and are flat in test |
| labels | `fwd`: y = 1 if `labels.forward_return(h).ret > 0`. `tb`: y = 1 if `labels.triple_barrier(h, pt 1.0, sl 1.0, vol_window 7d).ret > 0` (profit-take hit, or vertical barrier with a positive return) |

**Grid (the 24 configs, identical on both symbols):** timeframe x horizon h (bars) x label x cost control.

| tf | h (bars) | labels | cost control `lo` | cost control `hi` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15m | 4 (1h), 16 (4h) | fwd, tb | band 0.02, min_hold max(1, h/4), max 6 round trips/day | band 0.05, min_hold h, max 2 round trips/day |
| 1h | 4 (4h), 24 (1d) | fwd, tb | band 0.02, min_hold max(1, h/4), max 4 round trips/day | band 0.05, min_hold h, max 2 round trips/day |
| 4h | 6 (1d), 42 (1w) | fwd, tb | band 0.02, min_hold max(1, h/4), max 3 round trips/day | band 0.05, min_hold h, max 1 round trip/day |

Config names: `lr_<tf>_<label>_h<h>_<lo|hi>`, e.g. `lr_4h_tb_h42_hi`.

The smoke mode (`--smoke`) runs a few configs on 2019 (training-era data, nothing logged) to
check timing and plumbing only; it is not used to choose configs.

## Assessment (written 2026-11-05 after the single OOS run)

**It does not beat the best baseline, and it does not beat buy-and-hold.** The best config on
the weaker symbol, `lr_4h_fwd_h42_hi`, makes 0.63 (BTC) / 0.68 (ETH) net Sharpe at 5 bps, against
buy-and-hold 0.84 / 0.89 and the best rule baseline 1.35 / 1.47 (`ema_px_4h_180`,
`tsmom_4h_180`). The single best run, `lr_1h_tb_h24_lo` on ETH (0.85), is still below ETH
buy-and-hold. No run in the family beats buy-and-hold on both symbols. `promotion.evaluate` on
`lr_4h_fwd_h42_hi` (rebuilt with `build_candidate`, returns hashes match the registry) FAILs
on 12 (BTC) / 13 (ETH) criteria, including the random-timing permutation test (p = 0.58 / 0.56: the
trades show no timing skill) and DSR (~0 at N = 230).

**Where the "edge" comes from: the training window's drift, not timing.** Exposure is 45-71%
for the 1h/4h `hi` configs, the 2020-2021 return is most of the P&L, and the dominant
coefficients are slow trend/state terms (`ret_30d+`, `pxema_*`, `dd_30d`) plus a short-term
reversal term (`ret_h-`, `x_ret_h-`). With a rolling 24-month window the intercept learns the
recent base rate: `lr_4h_fwd_h42_hi` was long 98% of 2021, still long 77% of 2022 (the window was
still full of the 2020-2021 bull) and only 18% of BTC 2023 (the window was now full of 2022),
missing a +156% buy-and-hold year. It is a lagged base-rate filter that is wrong at both regime
turns, which is exactly where a 30-day EMA rule is right. The "gross P&L in trend" column is above
100% for 21 of 32 1h/4h runs: the model earns its money while price is above the 30-day EMA and gives
part of it back below it, i.e. it is a worse version of the baseline's trend filter.

**Disaster years.** Every 1h/4h run has a worst calendar year between -37% and -76%; for the four
best configs it is 2022 at -59% to -70% (buy-and-hold 2022: BTC -64%, ETH -68%), and MaxDD is -58% to
-89% on every 1h/4h run. The worst-year floor of -10% is met only by the 15m
`lo` configs that are flat ~99% of the time (`lr_15m_tb_h4_lo` ETH: worst year -0.2%,
MaxDD -11%, but 127 trades and 10% CAGR) and by `lr_15m_fwd_h4_lo` / `lr_15m_tb_h4_lo` on BTC
(-5.6% / -9.6%, Sharpe 0.15 / 0.17). 2026 H1 is negative for 7 of the 8 top-config runs
(`lr_1h_fwd_h4_hi` ETH: +1.7%).

**Cost and timeframe sensitivity.** The only gross timing signal is at 15m: short-horizon
reversal (`close_loc-`, `vwapdev_96-`, `z_4_2880-`) at thresholds of 0.60 gives gross Sharpe
1.16 / 1.41 for `lr_15m_fwd_h4_lo`, but only ~10-20 bps gross per trade, so 5 bps/side halves
it (0.15 / 0.69) and 10 bps/side makes it negative (-0.87 / -0.08). Going from 5 to 10 bps/side
costs 0.10-1.02 Sharpe at 15m, 0.10-0.58 for 1h `lo`, 0.06-0.32 for 1h `hi` and at most 0.10 at
4h (85-300 trades over 6.5 years): the 4h configs are cost-insensitive because they trade
rarely, but they have no timing edge to protect. The `hi` cost control (band 0.05,
min_hold = h, fewer round trips) beat `lo` in 18 of 24 config-symbol pairs at 5 bps. Triple-barrier and forward-return labels are
indistinguishable on this evidence. Ten runs clear 500 trades with a positive stress
Sharpe, but the best of them makes 0.68 at 5 bps.

**Verdict:** family FAILs. A linear model on these features does not add timing skill over
holding or over a 30-day trend rule after costs; it mostly re-learns the recent drift with a
lag. The 15m reversal signal is real gross but too thin for 5-10 bps/side taker costs.
