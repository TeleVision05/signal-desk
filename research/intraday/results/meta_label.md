# Meta-labeling on primary rules (family `meta_label`, kind `model`)

Script: `experiments/intraday/meta_label.py`. Long/flat spot, BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, walk-forward OOS
window [2020-01-01, 2026-07-01), every config logged at 5 and 10 bps/side with seed 7. Bars: 5m cache
resampled with `incomplete="drop"` (same frames as `rule_baselines`).

## Pre-registration (written 2026-11-05 before any OOS run)

24 configs per symbol (12 primaries x 2 secondary models), 48 runs in total (x2 costs). No config
will be added after seeing OOS results unless it is logged and marked `post_hoc` below.

Taken verbatim from `experiments/intraday/rule_baselines.py` (imported, not copied), so the primary
alone is exactly the logged baseline run of the same name.

### Events and labels

* A primary **trade** is one maximal run of bars with primary target 1: signal (decision) bar `s`
  (first bar of the run), exit decision bar `e` (first bar back at 0). As the simulator trades it:
  filled at `open[s+1]`, exited at `open[e+1]`.
* Label `y = 1` iff the net trade log return `log(open[e+1] / open[s+1]) - 2 x 5 bps` > 0 (round
  trip at base cost). `t1` = open time of bar `e+1` (when the label is known); a trade still open at
  the end of the data has no label (purged).
* The meta-labeled strategy takes the WHOLE primary trade (target 1 on bars `s..e-1`, exactly as the
  primary) iff `p > threshold` at bar `s`, otherwise stays flat for that trade. It never adds trades,
  sizes or exits of its own.

### Features at the signal bar `s` (all causal, timeframe-scaled; D = bars per day, W = 7D, M = 30D)

`features.make_feature_frame` with log returns (1, D, W, M), realized vol (D, W, M), return z-score
(h = D, window M), taker imbalance (1, D), VWAP deviation (D, W), volume ratio (D vs M), bar range /
close location, calendar (hour, day of week), cross-asset returns and spreads vs the other symbol
(1, D, W); plus derived: log(rv_D / rv_M), ret_W / (rv_W sqrt W), ret_M / (rv_M sqrt M); plus
**signal strength** (tsmom: ret_N / (rv_N sqrt N); Donchian: log(close / channel high) / rv_D;
mean reversion: the entry z-score); plus the primary's own track record known at `s`: previous
trade's net return, mean net return and win rate of the last 10 trades, log bars since the previous
exit (every earlier trade has exited by `open[s]`, so all are known).

### Secondary models (2, pre-registered)

| model | definition |
|---|---|
| logreg | median impute -> StandardScaler -> LogisticRegression(C = 0.1, L2, lbfgs, max_iter 1000) |
| lgbm | LGBMClassifier(n_estimators 100, learning_rate 0.05, num_leaves 7, max_depth 3, min_child_samples 20, subsample 0.8 (freq 1), colsample_bytree 0.8, reg_lambda 1, random_state 7, n_jobs 1) |

### Walk-forward, threshold and fallbacks

* `splits.walk_forward_splits` over the EVENT index (signal times) with the event `t1`, expanding
  training from 2017-09-01, monthly test blocks, retrained every month. Folds start at 2019-10-01 so
  trades opened in the last quarter of 2019 and still held on 2020-01-01 are decided by a causal
  model; trades opened before 2019-10-01 are not taken. Only [2020-01-01, 2026-07-01) is backtested.
* Threshold, per fold, from {0 (take every trade), 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65}: the one that
  maximises the SUM of net trade returns of the taken trades over out-of-fold probabilities from
  `splits.purged_kfold(train events, t1, n_splits = 5, within = fold)` (ties -> the lowest threshold).
  The model is then refit on all of the fold's training events and applied to the test month.
* Fewer than 40 labelled training events, or a single class: take every primary trade (the primary).
* Comparison: the primary alone on identical bars and window (it is the logged `rule_baselines` run;
  it is NOT re-logged under this family, the script checks the returns hash is already registered).

## Analysis (written by hand after the single logged run, 2026-11-05)

No `post_hoc` configs: the 24 pre-registered configs per symbol are the only ones run. That makes 48
runs, each logged at 5 and 10 bps/side. The registry held N = 180 runs after this run, counting other
families. The full grid took about 10 minutes with monthly retraining, so no coarsening was needed.
The "primary alone" column was not re-logged. The script checked that its base and stress returns
hashes are already in `rule_baselines.jsonl`, and they are. Re-logging identical returns under a
second family would have inflated N for everyone.

**Does it beat the best baseline and buy-and-hold on net Sharpe? No.** The best config on the weaker
symbol is `meta_logreg_tsmom_4h_180`: Sharpe 1.34 on BTC and 1.48 on ETH at 5 bps, 1.27 and 1.43 at
10 bps. It is the primary in disguise. Inner CV chose threshold 0 ("take every trade") in every BTC
fold and in all but 4% of ETH events, so it took 100% and 98% of the primary trades. On BTC it is
identical to `tsmom_4h_180` and 0.004 below the registry's best baseline (`ema_px_4h_180`, 1.348). On
ETH it is +0.016 above `tsmom_4h_180` (1.467), because it skipped a handful of trades, which is noise.
It beats buy-and-hold (Sharpe 0.84 on BTC, 0.89 on ETH), but so does the primary.
`promotion.evaluate` returns FAIL for it:

* 206 / 215 trades (needs 500)
* MaxDD -36% / -43%
* positive months 41% / 49%
* worst year -23% / -12%
* DSR about 0.000 at N = 180
* PBO 0.21 / 0.25
* below the best baseline on BTC

No meta config has a base Sharpe of at least 1.0 on both symbols except this one.

**Where the edge comes from.** The filter only does anything where the primary has zero or negative
expectancy after costs.

* **Slow trend primaries** (4h tsmom and Donchian, `tsmom_1h_168`, `donch_1h_168_48`): out-of-fold
  filtering never beat "take all", because trend P&L lives in a few right-tail trades that the
  features cannot recognise. OOS AUC was 0.19 to 0.58, centred on 0.5, so the threshold stayed at 0
  and the result equals the primary.
* **Where it did filter a trend primary, it hurt.** `meta_lgbm_tsmom_4h_180` on ETH skipped trades
  that averaged +496 bps. Its Sharpe fell from 1.47 to 1.32, and its 2020 return from 461% to 164%.
* **Mean reversion (15m, 1h) and the fast `donch_1h_24_24` / `tsmom_1h_24`:** the filter was active
  in nearly every fold and took 24-60% of trades. Taken trades beat skipped ones. For example,
  `meta_lgbm_mr_15m_k2` on ETH averaged +10.8 bps taken versus -16.4 bps skipped, with a 65% versus
  56% win rate, at AUC 0.53-0.59.
* **Effect on the worst primaries:** `mr_15m_k2` went from -0.86 / -0.61 to 0.27 / 0.94 (lgbm), and
  `mr_1h_k3` from 0.43 / 0.43 to 0.64 / 0.65.

So the selection signal is real but weak, at an AUC of about 0.55. The gain comes from avoiding
losing trades, not from finding big winners. The rescued configs have 1-2% exposure, CAGR of 3-19%,
a daily-P&L t-stat of about 1.7, and fewer than 500 trades or under 15 bps per trade. Across all 48
runs, 20 improved on their primary by more than 0.02 Sharpe, 7 got worse and 21 were unchanged.
Logreg and LightGBM are equal on average (+0.22 Sharpe each).

**Sensitivity to cost and timeframe.**

* **Trend configs barely move with cost** (`tsmom_4h_180` on BTC: 1.34 to 1.27, 232 to 222 net bps
  per trade).
* **Meta-filtered mean-reversion configs are cost-fragile.** `meta_*_mr_15m_k2` makes 1.5-12 bps per
  trade at 5 bps. At 10 bps its Sharpe is -0.61 to -0.63 on BTC and -0.23 to 0.17 on ETH.
  `mr_15m_k3` and `mr_1h_k3` hold up better (0.14-0.51 at stress, 13-48 bps per trade).
* **The labels are net of base cost only**, so at stress cost the filter is optimising the wrong
  hurdle.
* **By timeframe, the mean Sharpe change versus the primary** is +0.97 at 15m, +0.13 at 1h and -0.01
  at 4h. Meta-labeling helps only where the primary is fast and loses money after costs. At 4h it
  does nothing or does harm.

**Is any year a disaster?**

* **Best config:** its worst years are the primary's. BTC lost 23% in 2022 and ETH lost 12% in
  2026H1. Neither is a catastrophe, but both miss the -10% gate.
* **Rescued mean-reversion configs:** their P&L is concentrated in 2021 (+60% to +98% that year,
  roughly flat otherwise). Bad years include 2022 at -22% (`meta_logreg_mr_1h_k3`, BTC) and 2024 at
  -16% / -18% (`meta_lgbm_mr_1h_k3`). The best-month share is 36% on BTC and 51% on ETH, against a
  cap of 30%.
* **`meta_*_tsmom_1h_24` is a disaster:** worst years of -34% to -55% and MaxDD of -66% to -81%. The
  filter cut the losses but cannot make the primary viable.

**Verdict.** Meta-labeling with these features adds nothing on top of the trend primaries that carry
the baselines. It modestly rescues losing mean-reversion primaries, but only to a Sharpe below 1
with P&L concentrated in 2021. Nothing here should be promoted.
